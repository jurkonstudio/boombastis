Image Compressor

Shrink and convert images right in your browser.

How it works

This compressor resizes and re-encodes your images locally using your browser, so files never leave your device. WebP usually gives the smallest result; JPEG is the most compatible.

How to use

  1. Drop or choose one or more images.
  2. Pick a format, a maximum width and the quality.
  3. Download each result, or check the total size you saved.
