How it works

Your IP is detected with a request to ipify, and its country and ISP come from ipwho.is. DNS records are fetched with DNS over HTTPS directly from Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and Google (8.8.8.8) and shown side by side. Boombastis does not log or store anything.

How to use

  1. Open the page to see your public IP.
  2. Type a domain and press Look up.
  3. Compare the Cloudflare and Google answers.

Why does my IP differ from my device’s IP?

Your public IP is assigned by your internet provider or VPN. If you are connected to a VPN, you will see the VPN server’s address and location instead of your own.
