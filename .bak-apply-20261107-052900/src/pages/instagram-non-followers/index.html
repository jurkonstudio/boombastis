    About this tool

    This tool compares the people you follow on Instagram with the people who follow you, and shows who does not follow you back. It works from the data export that Instagram lets you download from your own account, so you never share your password and nothing is uploaded. It is useful for tidying a following list, checking a business or hobby account, or simply satisfying curiosity.

    How it works

    You choose the ZIP you downloaded from Instagram, or the followers and following files inside it. The page reads them in your browser, builds two lists of usernames and compares them. You then see three groups: accounts you follow that do not follow you back, accounts that follow you that you do not follow back, and mutual follows. Each username has an Open profile link, and you can search the list, copy it or download it as a CSV file.

    No account, login or email is needed, and the files never leave your device. Close the tab and the data is gone.

    How to use

    1. In Instagram, request your data: Accounts Center, Your information and permissions, Download your information.
    2. Select only Followers and following, choose JSON, and set the date range to All time.
    3. When Instagram tells you the file is ready, download it to your device.
    4. Choose the ZIP here, or select followers_1.json and following.json together.
    5. Open the Not following you back tab and review the list.

    Unfollowing safely

    This tool does not unfollow anyone for you, and that is intentional. Instagram may limit accounts that follow or unfollow many people very quickly, and tools that automate it often break the platform rules. Use the Open profile link on each account and unfollow by hand, a few at a time. Keep in mind that the export is a snapshot from the moment you requested it, so some accounts may have changed since then.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do you need my Instagram password?

    No. The tool never asks you to log in. You upload files that you downloaded from your own account, and they are read inside your browser. They are not sent to any server.

    How do I get my followers and following files?

    In the Instagram app, open your profile menu, go to Accounts Center, then Your information and permissions, then Download your information. Choose your profile, select only Followers and following, pick JSON as the format and All time as the date range, then submit the request. Instagram prepares the file and tells you when it is ready. Menu names can change slightly between app versions.

    Which files can I upload?

    Upload the ZIP you downloaded, or the files inside it: followers_1.json (and followers_2.json if you have more) and following.json. The HTML versions of the same files also work. If your browser cannot open ZIP files, unzip the download first.

    Why does it say no files were found?

    The tool looks for files named followers or followers_1 and following, ending in .json or .html. If you renamed the files or exported something else, such as only your posts, upload the correct ones.

    Why do the numbers differ from the Instagram app?

    The export is a snapshot taken when you requested it. Accounts that were deleted, deactivated or renamed since then, and people who followed or unfollowed you afterwards, will not match. Request a fresh export if you want current numbers.

    Can it show who unfollowed me?

    Not directly. The tool compares your followers list with your following list at one moment in time. It can tell you who you follow that does not follow you back, but not who left yesterday.

    Can it unfollow people for me?

    No, on purpose. Automatic unfollow tools break the rules of social networks and can get your account limited. Use the Open profile links and unfollow manually, a few at a time.

    Does it work for TikTok, X or Facebook?

    Not yet. This version supports the Instagram data export only, because each network uses a different file format.
