Base64 Encoder / Decoder

Convert text to Base64 and back, with UTF-8 and URL-safe support.

About this tool

Base64 turns binary or text data into plain ASCII characters so it can travel safely inside JSON, URLs, emails and HTML. This tool encodes and decodes text, including emoji and non-Latin scripts, by converting through UTF-8 first.

Tick URL-safe to use the - and _ alphabet without padding, as used in JWTs and many web APIs. Decoding accepts both the standard and URL-safe forms.

How to use

  1. Paste text to encode or a Base64 string to decode.
  2. Press Encode or Decode.
  3. Copy the result.

Frequently asked questions

Is Base64 encryption?

No. It is only an encoding and anyone can decode it. Never use it to protect secrets.

Why does decoding fail?

The input may contain characters outside the Base64 alphabet or have the wrong length. Remove spaces or quotes and try again.

What is URL-safe Base64?

A variant that replaces + and / with - and _ and drops the = padding so the value can sit in a URL.

Does it support emoji and accents?

Yes. Text is converted to UTF-8 bytes before encoding and back after decoding.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser.
