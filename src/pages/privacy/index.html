Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Summary

Boombastis does not ask you to create an account and does not use analytics. The site itself does not set cookies, but it shows ads through Google AdSense, which can set its own cookies (see the third-party list below). Files, passwords and text you enter into the tools are processed in your browser and are not sent to us.

Tools that stay on your device

Image Compressor, QR Code Generator, Password Generator, Password Strength Checker, Word Counter, Typing Speed Test, CPS Test and Reaction Time Test (the tests themselves), Hash Generator, Browser Info, JSON Formatter, Base64, Case Converter and the Indonesian tools (Terbilang, Pace, THR, Age, Photo Compressor, JSON/CSV) all work locally. Nothing you type or select leaves your device.

Third-party requests

  • IP & DNS Checker: asks api64.ipify.org and ipwho.is for your public IP address, country and ISP, and sends the domain names you look up to Cloudflare DNS (cloudflare-dns.com) and Google DNS (dns.google). Those services see your IP address and the domain you query, under their own privacy policies.
  • QR Code Generator: loads a QR library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Cloudflare receives a normal web request (IP address, browser details) when the page loads.
  • Advertising (Google AdSense): pages load a script from pagead2.googlesyndication.com to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this and other sites, and they receive your IP address and browser details. You can manage personalized ads at adssettings.google.com and read how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses as described in its own privacy statement. We do not receive that log data.

Typing Speed Test leaderboard

The typing test runs in your browser. Only if you press Submit score, the name you typed, your speed, accuracy, test length and the submission time are sent to our database (Supabase) and shown on the public leaderboard. Do not use your real name or any personal data as a leaderboard name. No account, email or IP address is stored by us for this feature. To have an entry removed, write to us from the Contact page.

CPS Test and Reaction Time Test leaderboards

Both tests run in your browser. Only if you press Submit score, the name you typed, your score (clicks per second, or average reaction time), the test length and the submission time are sent to our database (Supabase) and shown on the public leaderboard. Do not use your real name or any personal data as a leaderboard name. No account, email or IP address is stored by us for this feature. To have an entry removed, write to us from the Contact page.

Data stored in your browser

To work offline, the site stores its own files in your browser cache through a service worker. This contains no personal data. The tests also keep your best scores and the last leaderboard name in your browser storage so you can see them next time. You can remove it any time by clearing site data for {{HOST}} in your browser settings.

Changes

If we add analytics or other services, or change how ads work, we will update this page first and say what data they collect.
