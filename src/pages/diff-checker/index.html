About this tool

A diff shows the difference between two versions of a text: what was added and what was removed. Use it to review edits to an article, compare two copies of a config file, or check what changed in a contract.

Added text is highlighted in green and removed text in red with a line through it.

How to use

  1. Paste the old text on the left and the new text on the right.
  2. Pick Lines for documents and code, Words for prose, or Characters for tiny edits.
  3. Press Compare and read the highlights.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser with JavaScript, so your files never leave your device and nothing is stored on a server.

Which mode should I use?

Lines for code and lists, Words for paragraphs of writing, Characters for spotting a single changed letter.

Is there a size limit?

Very long texts are refused in word and character mode to keep your browser responsive. Use line mode for long files.

Can I ignore capital letters or extra spaces?

Yes. Tick Ignore case or Ignore spaces.
