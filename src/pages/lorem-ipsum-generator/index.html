About this tool

Lorem Ipsum is scrambled Latin used by designers and developers to fill a layout before real content exists, so people judge the design rather than the words.

Choose paragraphs, sentences or words, and tick the option to begin with the classic first line.

How to use

  1. Choose paragraphs, sentences or words.
  2. Enter the amount.
  3. Press Generate.
  4. Press Copy text and paste it into your design.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser with JavaScript, so your files never leave your device and nothing is stored on a server.

What does Lorem Ipsum mean?

It is a scrambled passage from a work by Cicero. It has no meaning, which is the point: it looks like text without distracting the reader.

Is the text random every time?

Yes. Press Generate again for a new variation.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes. The text is placeholder content with no copyright restrictions.
