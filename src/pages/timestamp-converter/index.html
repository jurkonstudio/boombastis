About this tool

Unix time counts the seconds since 1 January 1970 (UTC). Logs, APIs and databases use it everywhere, but it is hard to read. This tool converts it to a normal date and back.

It detects seconds and milliseconds automatically: a value below 100 billion is treated as seconds.

How to use

  1. Type a timestamp such as 1700000000 and press Convert timestamp.
  2. Or pick a date and time, choose the time zone, and press Convert date.
  3. Read the result in seconds, milliseconds, ISO 8601, UTC, your time zone and relative time.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser with JavaScript, so your files never leave your device and nothing is stored on a server.

How do I know if it is seconds or milliseconds?

Seconds have 10 digits today (1700000000), milliseconds 13 digits (1700000000000). The tool detects it for you.

Why is the time different from what I expected?

Unix time is always UTC. The local time shown depends on your device time zone.

What is the Year 2038 problem?

Systems that store seconds in a signed 32-bit number overflow on 19 January 2038. Modern systems use 64 bits.
