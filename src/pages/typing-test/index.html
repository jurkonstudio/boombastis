    About this tool

    This typing speed test measures how many words per minute you can type and how accurately you do it. Pick a 15, 30 or 60 second test, start typing the words on screen, and the timer begins with your first keystroke. When time is up you see your WPM, raw WPM, accuracy and the number of correct and wrong words.

    How it works

    The words are picked at random from a list of common English words, so every test is different. Each word you finish is checked when you press the space bar: a correct word adds its length to your score, a wrong word adds nothing. WPM is the number of correct characters divided by five and scaled to one minute, which is the standard method used by most typing tests.

    The test itself runs in your browser. If you choose to share a result, the leaderboard stores only the name you type, your speed, accuracy, test length and the time of submission. No account or email is needed.

    How to use

    1. Choose 15, 30 or 60 seconds.
    2. Click the text box and start typing the highlighted word. Press space after each word.
    3. Read your result when the timer ends.
    4. Optionally enter a name and submit your score to the leaderboard.

    Tips to type faster

    Keep your eyes on the screen instead of the keyboard, use all ten fingers, and aim for a steady rhythm before you push for speed. Practice a few minutes every day: short regular sessions improve speed and accuracy more than one long session.

    Frequently asked questions

    How is typing speed (WPM) calculated?

    WPM is the number of correctly typed characters divided by five, scaled to one minute. Five characters count as one word, so long and short words are treated fairly. Only words typed correctly count toward your score.

    What is a good typing speed?

    Many adults type around 40 words per minute. Reaching 60 to 80 WPM is comfortable for office work, and above 100 WPM is considered very fast. Accuracy matters as much as speed, because corrections cost time.

    What is the difference between WPM and raw WPM?

    Raw WPM counts every character you typed, right or wrong. WPM counts only the words you typed correctly, so it is always equal to or lower than raw WPM.

    How is accuracy measured?

    Accuracy is the share of your keystrokes that did not introduce a mistake in the current word. Fixing a typo with backspace still counts the original wrong keystroke.

    Is my score saved?

    Only if you press Submit score. Then your chosen name, speed, accuracy, test length and the time are stored in the public leaderboard. Nothing is saved when you just take the test, apart from your best score and name, which stay in your own browser.

    Can I take the test on my phone?

    Yes. Tap the text box and type on your on-screen keyboard. Results on a phone are usually lower than on a physical keyboard, so compare scores within the same device type.
