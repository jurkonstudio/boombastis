About this tool

A watermark marks a document as confidential, draft or yours. This tool places your text or logo on every page at the angle, size and transparency you choose, or repeats it across the page.

Text uses a bold standard font that supports Latin letters. For other alphabets or emoji, use the image option with a picture of your text.

How to use

  1. Choose the PDF.
  2. Pick Text or Image. Type the words or choose a PNG/JPG logo.
  3. Set size, opacity and angle. Tick Repeat to tile the watermark.
  4. Press Add watermark and download.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser with JavaScript, so your files never leave your device and nothing is stored on a server.

Can a watermark be removed later?

A watermark is drawn on top of the pages, so it is hard to remove cleanly. Keep your original file safe.

Does it support Arabic, Chinese or emoji text?

Not as text. Use the image option with a picture of your text or logo.

What size should my logo be?

A PNG with a transparent background works best. Use the size slider to scale it.
