About this tool

Whether you are writing an essay, a tweet or a product description, it helps to know your length. This word counter shows words, characters, characters without spaces and estimated reading time, and updates as you type. Your text stays in the page and is never sent anywhere.

How it works

Words are counted as groups of characters separated by whitespace. Characters are counted twice, with and without spaces, which matches the limits many platforms use. Reading time divides the word count by 200 words per minute, a common average for adults reading silently.

How to use

  1. Paste or type your text.
  2. Read the live word and character counts.
  3. Use the reading time to plan your content.

Frequently asked questions

How is the word count calculated?

The tool splits your text on spaces, tabs and line breaks and counts the groups. Hyphenated words such as well-known count as one word.

Why does the count look wrong for Chinese or Japanese?

Those languages do not put spaces between words, so a whitespace-based counter treats long stretches as a single word. Use the character count instead.

Do emoji count as one character?

Some emoji are made of two or more code units, so they may count as more than one character. Most platforms with character limits count them in a similar way, but always check the platform’s own counter.

What reading speed is used?

200 words per minute, rounded to the nearest minute with a minimum of one minute for any text. Technical or dense writing is usually read more slowly.

Is my text saved?

No. The text exists only in your browser tab. Close the page and it is gone.
