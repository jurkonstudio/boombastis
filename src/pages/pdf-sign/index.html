About this tool

Sign PDF lets you add a signature image to a contract, form or letter without printing or scanning. Draw it with your finger, type your name in a handwriting style, or upload a photo of your real signature.

This adds a visual signature image. It is not a certified digital signature with a legal certificate, but it is accepted for many everyday documents. Check the rules for your case.

How to use

  1. Choose the PDF to sign.
  2. Create your signature: draw it, type your name, or upload a picture.
  3. Pick the page, then tap or drag on the preview to place the signature. Use the slider to resize it.
  4. Choose This page, All pages, Last page or First page, then press Sign and download.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser with JavaScript, so your files never leave your device and nothing is stored on a server.

Is this a legally binding digital signature?

It places an image of your signature on the page. This is fine for many everyday agreements, but it is not a certificate-based digital signature. Check what your document needs.

Can I sign more than one page?

Yes. Choose All pages to stamp the signature at the same spot on every page.

How do I get a clean uploaded signature?

Sign on white paper with a dark pen, take a bright, flat photo, and keep Make the white background transparent ticked.
