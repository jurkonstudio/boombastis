About this tool

This converter handles the units people use every day: kilometers and miles, kilograms and pounds, Celsius and Fahrenheit, liters and gallons, hectares and acres, km/h and mph, and bytes up to terabytes.

The table under the result shows your value in every unit of the category at once. Digital storage includes both decimal (KB, MB, GB) and binary (KiB, MiB, GiB) units.

How to use

  1. Choose a category such as Length.
  2. Type the value and choose the From and To units.
  3. Read the answer, or scan the table for other units.
  4. Use Swap units to reverse the direction.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser with JavaScript, so your files never leave your device and nothing is stored on a server.

Why is a GB different from a GiB?

A gigabyte (GB) is 1,000,000,000 bytes. A gibibyte (GiB) is 1,073,741,824 bytes. Storage makers use GB, while many operating systems report in GiB.

Are US and UK gallons the same?

No. This tool uses the US gallon (3.785 liters).

How accurate is it?

Results are shown with up to 10 significant digits.
