About this tool

Organize PDF shows each page as a thumbnail. Rotate sideways scans, delete blank or unwanted pages and change the page order, then save a clean new file.

Everything happens on your device. Thumbnails are drawn by your browser and the final PDF is rebuilt from your original pages, so text stays selectable.

How to use

  1. Choose a PDF. Wait a moment while thumbnails load.
  2. Use ⟲ and ⟳ to rotate, 🗑 to remove (↩ restores), ◀ and ▶ to move a page. On a computer you can also drag a page to a new spot.
  3. Press Save PDF and download the new file.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser with JavaScript, so your files never leave your device and nothing is stored on a server.

Can I reorder pages on a phone?

Yes. Use the ◀ and ▶ buttons under each page. Drag and drop works on computers.

Does rotating change the quality?

No. Rotation is stored in the PDF page settings and nothing is re-rendered.

Is there a page limit?

No fixed limit, but very long PDFs take longer to draw thumbnails.
