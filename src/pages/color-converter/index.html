About this tool

Designers and developers move colors between HEX (#ff3b30), RGB (255, 59, 48) and HSL (6, 100%, 59%). This tool keeps all three in sync, so edit any one and the others update.

The contrast line tells you whether white or black text is readable on your color, based on the WCAG AA rule that normal text needs a ratio of at least 4.5:1.

How to use

  1. Use the picker, or type a HEX, RGB or HSL value.
  2. The other formats update instantly.
  3. Check the contrast notes before using the color behind text.
  4. Tap Copy next to the value you need, or tap a shade to use it.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser with JavaScript, so your files never leave your device and nothing is stored on a server.

What is HSL?

Hue (0 to 360 degrees on the color wheel), saturation and lightness in percent. It is easy to make a color lighter or darker by changing only the last number.

Does it support short HEX like #f00?

Yes. Three-digit HEX is expanded to six digits.

What is a good contrast ratio?

WCAG AA asks for 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. AAA asks for 7:1.
