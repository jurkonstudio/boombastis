About Boombastis

Free online tools that stay private.

What Boombastis is

Boombastis is an independent collection of free, single-purpose web tools: image and photo compression, QR codes, passwords, hashes, JSON and Base64, word counting, and a growing set of calculators for Indonesian users (terbilang, THR, running pace, age and more). There is no sign-up and no cost.

How it works

Almost every tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Images, files, passwords and text are processed on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. After your first visit, the site can also work offline. The IP & DNS Checker is the exception because it has to look up information on the internet; see the Privacy page for exactly what it contacts.
