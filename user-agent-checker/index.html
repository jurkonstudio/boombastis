How it works

Everything on this page is read from your own browser with standard web APIs. Nothing is sent to Boombastis or anyone else.

What is a user agent?

The user agent is a text string your browser sends with every request. Websites use it, together with other signals, to adapt layouts and detect your browser and device. It is not a precise or private identifier.

Why is this useful?

  1. Include it when reporting a bug.
  2. Check what a site can learn about you.
  3. Confirm your VPN or privacy settings did not change your browser details.
